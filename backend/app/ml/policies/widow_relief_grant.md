# Widow Relief Grant Policy (Simulated)

## Eligibility
Applicants must be 18 or older, live in Sindh or Punjab, be widowed, and have a monthly household income at or below the published cap (Rs 30,000 in rule version 1). Rules are versioned; the version used for each decision is recorded in the decision trace.

## Required Documents
A clear photo of the original CNIC (front). The photo must be sharp, well lit and without glare. If the photo is unclear the applicant is asked to retake it; this is never a rejection.

## Name Spelling Differences
Differences such as Muhammad, Mohammad, Muhammed or Mohd between the application and official records are common. Borderline matches are sent to an officer for verification instead of being rejected automatically.

## Decisions And Appeals
The system can recommend approval, ask for more proof, or send the case to a human officer. Only an officer can reject, and a rejection always includes a written reason. Applicants may appeal any officer decision.

## Grant Amount
The grant is Rs 25,000 per quarter, paid at authorised campsites or partner banks.
