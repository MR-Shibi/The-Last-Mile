# Scam Awareness Policy (Simulated)

## No Fee Is Ever Charged
Official relief programmes never ask beneficiaries to pay any fee, tax, registration charge, processing fee or security deposit to receive a grant. Any message asking you to send money, JazzCash, Easypaisa or a bank transfer before receiving relief is a scam.
Urdu: سرکاری امدادی پروگرام کبھی کوئی فیس، ٹیکس یا رجسٹریشن چارجز نہیں لیتا۔ رقم مانگنے والا پیغام دھوکہ ہے۔

## Never Share PIN Or OTP
Officials will never ask for your ATM PIN, OTP code, verification code or password by SMS or phone call. Do not share these codes with anyone, including people claiming to be agents.
Urdu: کوئی سرکاری اہلکار آپ کا اے ٹی ایم پن یا او ٹی پی کوڈ نہیں مانگتا۔

## Official Sender And Links
Official programme messages come only from the short code 8171 (simulated in this prototype). Messages from ordinary mobile numbers, shortened links (bit.ly, tinyurl) or unknown websites are not official. Official notices never ask you to click a link to claim money.

## Urgency And Lucky Draw Lures
Messages saying "lucky draw", "you have won", "final notice" or "expires in 2 hours" are pressure tactics used by fraudsters. Relief is based on eligibility surveys, not prizes.

## How To Report Fraud
Report suspicious messages to the toll-free helpline 0800-26477 (simulated) or show the message at your nearest Scam Shield shop. Do not reply or call back the sender.
