# Payment Collection Policy (Simulated)

## Where Payments Are Made
Grant payments are made only at authorised campsites and partner bank branches, after biometric verification. Bring your original CNIC. Payment is free of charge.

## Payment Notifications
When a quarterly instalment is ready, an SMS is sent from 8171 with the amount and collection instructions. The message never contains a link and never requests money.

## Officer Confirmation
Even when the system recommends approval, a department officer confirms every disbursement with one tap. The AI system can recommend but can never send money or reject an application on its own.

## Failed Payments
If the payment switch fails, the payment is queued for retry and the beneficiary is informed. No action or fee is required from the beneficiary.
