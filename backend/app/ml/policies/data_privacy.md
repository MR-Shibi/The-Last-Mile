# Data Privacy And Consent Policy (Simulated)

## Consent Is Off By Default
Citizens choose which data can be shared: identity, income, documents and helper access. Nothing is shared until the citizen turns a permission on.

## Withdrawing Consent
A citizen can withdraw consent at any time from the "Who Has My Data" screen. Withdrawal takes effect immediately: officers and helpers lose access on their very next request.

## Community Helpers
A community helper may assist up to 40 families on one shared phone. Each family has its own PIN and its own consent; switching families clears the screen and is recorded in the audit log.

## Audit Trail
Every access, consent change and decision is written to a tamper-evident, hash-chained audit log that auditors can verify at any time.
